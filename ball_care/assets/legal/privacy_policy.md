1\. Overview



This Privacy Policy explains how B.M.T. — Ball Maintenance Tracker (“B.M.T.”, “the App”, or “we”) collects, uses, and protects user information.

B.M.T. is designed primarily for offline use and does not require account creation or internet access for its core functionality.



2\. Information We Collect



B.M.T. does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) directly from users.

However, the following limited data may be handled by third-party services integrated into the App:



Advertisements (Google AdMob): Device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostic information may be collected to serve relevant ads.



In-App Purchases: Basic transaction information (non-personal) may be processed securely by Google Play or the App Store.



Analytics (if enabled): Anonymous app usage statistics may be collected to improve performance.



All locally entered user data (such as bowling ball logs, maintenance history, and notes) is stored only on the user’s device unless explicitly exported by the user.



3\. Third-Party Content and Data Sources



B.M.T. may display bowling ball data and images obtained from bowwwl.com, used with permission from that site’s owner.

This information is strictly informational and is not collected from users.

No user data is shared with or transmitted to bowwwl.com or any other third-party data provider.



All third-party content remains the property of its original source and may be subject to separate terms of use.

B.M.T. is not responsible for the content, policies, or accuracy of third-party data providers.



4\. Advertising and Monetization



The App may show third-party advertisements via Google AdMob.

These ad providers may use cookies or unique identifiers to deliver ads and measure performance.

Users can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting device ad settings.



Users may also make an in-app purchase to remove ads. Such purchases are processed securely by the respective app store and are not handled directly by B.M.T.



5\. Data Storage and Retention



All user-generated data is stored locally on the device.



No data is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers by B.M.T.



Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data.
